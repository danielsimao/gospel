# Image prompts: Tem volta?

Same rules as `docs/graphics/PROMPTS.md`, the site's own prompt file:

- One prompt per fresh chat.
- Every block opens with "Generate a new image."
- Copy only what sits between ▼ and ▲.
- Ask for the largest PNG, and send results raw.

Treatment happens in code: violet tint, darkening, opacity, crops.

One rule is specific to this campaign: **nothing in an image may look like Volta.** No logos, no letters, no "V" shapes, no recycling arrows, no brand colours, no store signage. We borrow the ritual, never the brand.

The pages already leave a slot for each image. Drop the files into `prototypes/volta/assets/` with the names below.

---

## 1 · A ranhura: the machine's slot at night

**Ships as:** `assets/slot.png`, the landing background and story frame 1, at about 20% opacity under the violet.

**Why:** the opening everyone in Portugal now feeds bottles into. Shown empty and dark, it reads as a question: what does it accept?

**▼ COPY FROM HERE ▼**

```
Generate a new image.

A photograph, close up, of the round intake opening of a reverse vending machine for bottles and cans, seen straight on at night. The opening is a dark circular hole set in a smooth matte panel. A faint cool light glows from deep inside the hole. Everything outside the opening falls into near-black.

Colour: near-black overall, with the panel a very dark grey. The inner light is pale and cool, not blue, not green. Low saturation.

Composition: portrait 9:16, the opening slightly above centre, lots of dark empty space below it for text. No border, no vignette frame.

Do not include: any logo, letters, numbers, symbols, the letter V, recycling arrows, stickers, labels, screens with text, store signage, people, hands, bottles or cans, or lens flare.

Style: photographic, high contrast, sharply focused, not illustration, not a 3D render.
```

Generate a new image.

A photograph of a single empty aluminium drinks can, badly dented and crumpled on one side, lying on its side on a smooth dark surface. The can is completely plain, bare brushed metal with no print, no label and no colour.

Colour: near-black background, the can in cold neutral grey and silver with soft highlights. Fully desaturated.

## 3 · O talão: a blank receipt

**Ships as:** `assets/receipt.png`, story frame 3 and the back of the tract, as texture.

**Why:** the receipt is the campaign's object. The real text is set in code, so the image must be blank.

**▼ COPY FROM HERE ▼**

```
Generate a new image.

A photograph of a single long strip of blank thermal receipt paper, slightly curled at both ends, lying on a near-black surface. The paper is completely blank, with no printing at all. The bottom edge is torn in a fine zigzag.

Colour: the paper is a cool off-white, very slightly grey. The background is near-black. Low saturation, no warm cast.

Composition: portrait 9:16, the receipt running diagonally from upper left to lower right, filling about half the frame.

Do not include: any printed text, numbers, barcode, QR code, logo, symbols, the letter V, hands, people, other objects, or lens flare.

Style: photographic, high contrast, sharply focused, not illustration, not a 3D render.
```
