    To someone I'll probably never meet

    1 minute

    I'm writing this on the assumption that we'll never meet, and that you'll read it once. So I won't waste your time, and I won't pretend it's light reading. It's about the one appointment nobody misses.

    I'm going to ask you a few questions on the way. Tap your answers. They stay on this device. Nobody sees them, me included.

    Your answers stay on this device.

    Then let me ask you something first. If you saw a building, how would you know someone built it? You wouldn't need to meet the builder. The building is the proof.

    [[BSB:Hebrews 3:4]]

    Hebrews 3:4

    A painting tells you there's a painter. Creation tells you there's a Creator, and the Bible says that is plain enough to leave all of us without an excuse.

    [[BSB:Romans 1:20]]

    Romans 1:20

    If you're not persuaded, read on anyway. You have a conscience, and that is where this letter goes next.

    I

    Your answer

    1 minute

    Say there is a heaven, and you're standing at its door. If God asked why He should let you in, what would you say?

    Hold onto that answer. I'll come back to it at the end.

    One more, and it's the one this whole letter hangs on.

    II

    The standard

    4 minutes

    Most of us measure ourselves against other people, and by that measure we usually come out fine. God doesn't use that measure. He gave us His Law, and He wrote the point of it on every conscience. You already know some things are wrong without being told.

    [[BSB:Romans 2:15]]

    Romans 2:15

    Here are six of the Ten Commandments. Answer each one honestly. Nobody is keeping score but you.

    III

    By your own answers

    2 minutes

    Here is half a sentence from the book of Hebrews. I'll give you the other half later.

    [[BSB:Hebrews 9:27]]

    Hebrews 9:27

    Answer the three questions above to go on.

    IV

    But God

    2 minutes

    The sentence doesn't end at judgment

    [[BSB:Hebrews 9:27]] [[BSB:Hebrews 9:28|so also Christ was offered once to bear the sins of many]].

    Hebrews 9:27–28

    Picture a courtroom. You are standing before a judge, guilty, and the fine is far more than you could ever pay.

    [[BSB:Romans 6:23|For the wages of sin is death]]

    Romans 6:23

    The judge is good, and that is the problem. A good judge can't look at a guilty person and let them walk free. A judge who did that would be corrupt.

    [[BSB:Proverbs 17:15]]

    Proverbs 17:15

    Then someone you've never met steps forward and pays your fine in full. Now the judge can dismiss your case, and do it legally, because justice has been done.

    [[BSB:Colossians 2:14]]

    Colossians 2:14

    That someone is Jesus.

    [[BSB:Romans 5:8]]

    Romans 5:8

    God became a man. He lived the life you couldn't live, died the death you deserved, and rose from the dead on the third day. The punishment you were owed fell on Him.

    [[BSB:Isaiah 53:5-6]]

    Isaiah 53:5–6

    [[BSB:2 Corinthians 5:21]]

    2 Corinthians 5:21

    [[BSB:1 Corinthians 15:3-4|For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that He was buried, that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures]].

    1 Corinthians 15:3–4

    Why would He do that? Scripture gives one reason.

    V

    Turn, and put Him on

    2 minutes

    God asks two things of you, and neither of them earns anything. They receive what He already paid.

    The first is to repent. That means turning from your sin. Feeling bad about it isn't the same thing. You can regret something and keep doing it.

    [[BSB:Mark 1:15|Repent and believe in the gospel!]]

    Mark 1:15

    [[BSB:Proverbs 28:13]]

    Proverbs 28:13

    [[BSB:2 Corinthians 7:10]]

    2 Corinthians 7:10

    The second is to trust Jesus alone. Think of a parachute. You can believe everything about it, that it is well made and that it works, and still not be wearing it. Trust means putting it on.

    [[BSB:Romans 13:14|clothe yourselves with the Lord Jesus Christ]]

    Romans 13:14

    [[BSB:Ephesians 2:8-9]]

    Ephesians 2:8–9

    There are no special words. After his adultery, King David prayed like this. You don't have to use his words, but you might find they say what you mean.

    Use your own words. He hears them.

    So I'll ask you plainly, and every answer here is a real one.

    VI

    Examine yourself

    2 minutes

    Paul wrote this to people in a church, not to outsiders. Checking yourself is a normal part of the Christian life. It isn't suspicion.

    [[BSB:2 Corinthians 13:5]]

    2 Corinthians 13:5

    Jesus said many people will stand before Him pointing to what they did for Him. What they lacked was Him.

    [[BSB:Matthew 7:21-23]]

    Matthew 7:21–23

    And He tells us what the Father's will is. It isn't a list of achievements.

    [[BSB:John 6:40|For it is My Father’s will that everyone who looks to the Son and believes in Him shall have eternal life]]

    John 6:40

    In the parable of the sower, some seed lands on rocky ground. It springs up at once, with joy, and then withers. Some lands among thorns and gets choked. Only the good soil bears fruit. A joyful moment was never the evidence.

    [[BSB:Mark 4:16-17]]

    Mark 4:16–17

    [[BSB:Mark 4:18-19]]

    Mark 4:18–19

    John wrote a whole letter so that believers could know where they stand. These are the marks he gives.

      None of this is a call to be perfect. The same letter says so.

      1 John 2:1

      John isn't asking whether you still sin. He is asking whether you defend your sin or confess it. Christ is what saves you. A changed life is the evidence that He has.

      Luke 6:44
