Rules every variant keeps

What can't change between lengths

Law before grace

No cross, forgiveness or comfort appears before the reader's own verdict. Objections raised during the Law are parked, not debated.

Testimony, never a shortcut

"No, I'm not a good person" and "Jesus paid for me" still go through every commandment. The answers are quoted back; they never shorten the Law.

Three equal exits

I'm turning to Him now · I need to think · I don't believe this. They look identical, are all on screen together, and none is preselected. There is no confirm step.

No decisionism

No "pray this to be saved", no "you're saved", no "welcome to the family". After the exits every promise is conditional: "If you have turned and trusted Him…".

Mortality is a stake, not a lever

Hebrews 9:27 appears once, in the Law half. Nothing about death or deadlines appears from repent and trust onwards.

No verse typed by hand

Every quotation is a placeholder filled from the Berean Standard Bible by the build, which fails on any reference that doesn't resolve.

Prototype limits

What a live build would add

A staffed volunteer line

"Talk to a real person" is a design only. It needs a WhatsApp rota of trained volunteers before it can go live.

Counting without tracking

A live build would count, cookieless and in aggregate: who reached the cross, per length; which exit was chosen; which take-away was used. Nothing per person.

Printing and calendar files

Inside this viewer, pages can't print or download a .ics file. Both work when the pages are opened in a normal browser tab. Here, Google Calendar and the WhatsApp message stand in.
